## What to build

A function that turns one line of a reading list into a book. A line looks like `The Salt Road | Ines Okafor | 1987`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `src/parse.js` exports `parseLine(line)`, which returns `{ title, author, year }`, with spaces trimmed and `year` as a number
- [ ] `parseLine` returns `null` for a line without exactly three parts, a blank part, or a year that isn't a whole number
- [ ] `tests/parse.test.js` covers a good line and each kind of bad line
